Ananya Sen • Banglami

Banglami was conceived out of an obsessive love for Bengal's tactile spirit—the vivid palette of Dhaka's rickshaw paintings, Kalighat patachitra strokes, and the cinematic mist of dawn across the Hooghly.

We reject clinical, overly-orchestrated documentation. Instead, our lens catches real cadence: the tear rolling past sandalwood bridal art, the burst of resin incense during Sandhi Puja, and the quiet dignity of old North Kolkata verandahs. Every frame is graded with archival tones that honor memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel outside Kolkata?

Yes. More than 40% of our commissions take place in Santiniketan, Darjeeling, Murshidabad, Delhi, and destination heritage venues abroad.

How soon do we receive the full heirloom gallery?

A 40-image sneak peek arrives within 72 hours of your wedding. Your final color-graded high-resolution gallery is delivered within 4–6 weeks.

Can we arrange physical album viewings at the studio?

Absolutely. We invite you to our Southern Avenue studio to hold our artisanal handloom-bound photobooks over freshly brewed Darjeeling first-flush tea.

Do you also provide cinematic wedding films?

Yes, our bespoke film team uses cinematic 4K Super 35 cameras, focusing on analog audio textures, shehnai notes, and documentary-style storytelling.
